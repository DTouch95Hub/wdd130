Haiti

The Citadelle Laferrière is a massive stone fortress in northern Haiti, built in the early 1800s by King Henri Christophe to protect against French invasion. Perched high on a mountain, it's the largest fortress in the Americas and a powerful symbol of Haitian independence. Today, it's a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a popular historic attraction.
