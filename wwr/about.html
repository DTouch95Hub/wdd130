Haiti Rafting Company

Experience the thrill of Haiti’s stunning rivers with expert guides dedicated to your safety and fun. From beginners to seasoned rafters, our tours offer unforgettable adventures surrounded by breathtaking nature.

History

Founded in 2015, Haiti Rafting Company began as a passion project by local adventurers aiming to share the country’s natural beauty through safe and exciting rafting experiences. Over the years, we have grown into the leading rafting provider in the region, committed to sustainable tourism and exceptional customer service.
